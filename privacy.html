This Privacy Policy describes how SwimBase ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application and related services (collectively, the "Services"). By using SwimBase, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

SwimBase operates on an "offline-first" basis, meaning core features can be used locally without an account. However, if you choose to sign in or use online features (such as cloud synchronization, coaching, or social features), we collect:

  • Account & Profile Information: Email address, name, and securely hashed passwords.
  • Health & Training Data: Information regarding your fitness activities, workout history, body metrics, and health or medical details (such as injuries or physical conditions) provided voluntarily to personalize training.
  • Social & Coaching Data: If you use the team, coaching, or friends features, we process data related to your team memberships, friend connections, and shared training results.
  • Communications: Messages sent through the in-app team or group chat features.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our Services and fitness programs.
  • Manage user accounts, authentication, and secure data storage via Supabase.
  • Enable social and coaching features, allowing coaches to view athlete results/workouts and facilitating peer connections via the friends feature.
  • Power in-app messaging and group chats between teammates and coaches.
  • Personalize workout plans based on your fitness goals and reported physical limitations.

3. Data Storage and Infrastructure

  • Backend Services: We use Supabase to securely store user authentication data, profile information, workout logs, and chat messages. Supabase employs industry-standard encryption and security measures.
  • Hosting: Web elements and documentation are hosted via GitHub Pages or Vercel.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Data may be shared only:

  • Within Teams/Friends: If you explicitly join a team or add friends, your coach or approved friends may view your profile, training history, and results as permitted by your in-app choices. They never gain access to your private account credentials.
  • Service Providers: With trusted infrastructure providers (such as Supabase) bound by strict confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal Requirements: If required by law or valid public authority requests.

5. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR, CCPA, and Regional Laws)

You have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your account and all associated cloud-stored data directly through the app or by contacting us.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to individuals under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this policy periodically. We will post any changes on this page with an updated revision date.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us via our support channels or repository page.
